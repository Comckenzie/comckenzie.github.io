Who is Charlie McKenzie?

I'm Charlie, a freelance composer for film and games. I started creating music with GarageBand back when I was a child, later becaming self-taught with Logic Pro. Since then, I've created hundreds of tracks, either through clients or simply for my own personal enjoyment. Because all my work is so varied, I've learned how to create a wide variety of different styles.

I moved to Chico State University to pursue a music degree studying music theory, sound design, and recording arts while also performing as a pianist in various bands. My goal is to compose for indie games and film projects that care about atmosphere and having a unique identity.
