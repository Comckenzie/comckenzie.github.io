Who is Charlie McKenzie?

Charlie McKenzie is a composer focused on music for video games and visual media. He started making video game music back in 2016, which slowly turned into a deep passion for creating interactive sound, atmosphere, and story through music.

Charlie moved to Chico State University to pursue a music degree studying music theory, sound design, and recording arts while also performing as a pianist in various bands. His work leans toward electronic, RPG, and retro-inspired styles, but is always chasing whatever sound best serves the world and emotion of a project. His goal is to compose for indie games and film projects that care about mood, detail, and identity.
