Charlie McKenzie

Composer For Games And Film

I'm Charlie McKenzie, a composer for visual media. This includes films, games, and smaller passion projects. I've been making video game music with Logic Pro since 2016, and have developoed a skillset of multiple different styles. I studied music theory, sound design, and recording arts at California State University, Chico, and I bring that background into every project I take on. I also took a summer school course for Utrecht Summer School, where I learned how to score for films.

I'm most skilled with chiptune, orchestral scoring, dark electronic, and RPG-style arrangements, but I can adapt to whatever best serves the world and emotion of your project. I'm currently open for commissions worldwide. If you're building a game and need a composer, get in touch.
